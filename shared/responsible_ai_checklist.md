# Responsible AI checklist

- State that the data is historical and educational.
- Do not claim clinical validity or diagnostic capability.
- Document the source, population, time period, and known sampling limitations.
- Check class imbalance and report metrics beyond accuracy.
- Check performance across available demographic groups where sample sizes permit.
- Prevent patient/encounter leakage during splitting.
- Explain missingness and whether it may encode care-access or workflow differences.
- Report uncertainty and avoid unsupported causal claims.
- Document what would be required before real-world deployment.
